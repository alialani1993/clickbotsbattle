How to play
  • Your bot is on the left. Click it to repair.
  • Enemy bot is on the right. Click it to attack.
  • Crystal in the middle. Click it to mine resources.
  • Spend resources on Bronze / Silver / Gold upgrades. Offers are random and re-roll after each purchase.
  • Destroy the enemy, or have the most HP when the timer ends.
  • The bars under the crystal compare what you and your opponent are doing (damage, repair, mining). Striped = auto-clickers.
  • green dots are your clicks, red dots are your opponent's.
